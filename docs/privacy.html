PhoneProof — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026 · applies to version 0.1.0

The short version. PhoneProof has no account, no server and no analytics. It inspects the phone it is installed on and keeps the results on that phone. Nothing you test is sent anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us, because there is nowhere for it to go. The app has no backend.

What it reads from the phone, in order to inspect it:

  • Model, manufacturer, chipset, Android version and security patch date
  • Storage size and free space
  • The list of sensors present
  • Display resolution and supported refresh rates
  • Whether any app holds device-administrator control
  • Whether the phone shows signs of root or an unlocked bootloader
  • Which parts of the screen respond to touch during the touch test

All of it stays in the app's private storage on the device. It is not transmitted, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app cannot read

Some things are worth naming because people reasonably assume an inspection app reads them. It does not, and on modern Android it cannot: your IMEI or serial number (restricted to system apps since Android 10), your contacts, your messages, your call log, your photos, your location, and your accounts.

Permissions

Version 0.1.0 requests no runtime permissions at all and has no internet permission. If a future version adds the camera — for reading an IMEI from a box label, for example — it will be requested at the moment it is used, with the reason on screen, and this page will be updated first.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising and no advertising SDK. When ads are introduced, Google's advertising identifier will leave the device as part of serving them, this page will be updated to say so before that version ships, and the app's own wording will change from "nothing leaves this device" to "your test results stay on this device". We would rather under-claim privacy than overstate it.

Backups

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off in the app's manifest, so inspection reports are not copied off the device by Android's backup system either.

Children

PhoneProof is a tool for buying second-hand phones and is not directed at children.

Changes

This page is versioned alongside the app in the public repository, so every change to it is visible in the commit history.

Contact
